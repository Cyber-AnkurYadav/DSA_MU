Definition:
-----------
Operators are special symbols used to perform mathematical, logical, and 
relational operations between operands (values or variables).

================================================================================
                         ARITHMETIC OPERATORS
================================================================================

Arithmetic operators are used to perform basic mathematical calculations:

1. ADDITION (+)
   - Purpose: Performs the sum of two operands
   - Example: a + b = sum of a and b
   - Use Case: Calculating totals, accumulating values

2. SUBTRACTION (-)
   - Purpose: Performs subtraction between two operands
   - Example: a - b = difference of a and b
   - Use Case: Finding difference, decrementing values

3. MULTIPLICATION (*)
   - Purpose: Finds the product of two operands
   - Example: a * b = product of a and b
   - Use Case: Scaling values, area calculations

4. DIVISION (/)
   - Purpose: Divides the first operand by the second, gives quotient
   - Example: a / b = quotient of a divided by b
   - Use Case: Splitting values, ratios

5. MODULUS (%)
   - Purpose: Returns the remainder after division
   - Example: a % b = remainder when a is divided by b
   - Use Case: Checking even/odd, cycling through values
